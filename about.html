About Go

Go is an abstract board game for two players, originating in ancient China. It's rules are very simple, yet lead to immensely complex situations. The simplicity of it's rules allows for a wide range of different styles of play, making every game feel completely different.

Despite having a relatively small presence in the UK, Go is one of the most popular board games in the world with over 46 million players.
